# Dual-tree descent: references

## N-body and tree codes (cluster-cluster interaction)

- Appel, A. W. *An efficient program for many-body simulation.* SIAM J. Sci. Stat. Comput. 6(1), 85–103, 1985.
- Barnes, J. and Hut, P. *A hierarchical O(N log N) force-calculation algorithm.* Nature 324, 446–449, 1986. (particle-cell only)
- Greengard, L. and Rokhlin, V. *A fast algorithm for particle simulations.* J. Comput. Phys. 73, 325–348, 1987.
- Greengard, L. *The Rapid Evaluation of Potential Fields in Particle Systems.* MIT Press, 1988. (general reference on the fast multipole method)
- Callahan, P. B. and Kosaraju, S. R. *A decomposition of multidimensional point sets with applications to k-nearest-neighbors and n-body potential fields.* J. ACM 42(1), 67–90, 1995. (conference version: STOC 1992, 546–556)
- Dehnen, W. *A hierarchical O(N) force calculation algorithm.* J. Comput. Phys. 179(1), 27–42, 2002.

## Panel clustering and hierarchical matrices (block cluster tree)

- Hackbusch, W. and Nowak, Z. P. *On the fast matrix multiplication in the boundary element method by panel clustering.* Numer. Math. 54, 463–491, 1989.
- Hackbusch, W. *A sparse matrix arithmetic based on H-matrices. Part I: Introduction to H-matrices.* Computing 62, 89–108, 1999.
- Grasedyck, L. and Hackbusch, W. *Construction and arithmetics of H-matrices.* Computing 70, 295–334, 2003.
- Börm, S., Grasedyck, L., and Hackbusch, W. *Introduction to hierarchical matrices with applications.* Eng. Anal. Bound. Elem. 27(5), 405–422, 2003.
- Hackbusch, W. *Hierarchical Matrices: Algorithms and Analysis.* Springer, 2015. Chapter 5, pp. 83–116 (block cluster trees: Sections 5.5–5.7, pp. 107–116).

## Collision detection and spatial joins (bounding-volume hierarchy pairs)

- Hubbard, P. M. *Collision detection for interactive graphics applications.* IEEE Trans. Visualization and Computer Graphics 1(3), 218–230, 1995.
- Palmer, I. J. and Grimsdale, R. L. *Collision detection for animation using sphere-trees.* Computer Graphics Forum 14(2), 105–116, 1995.
- Gottschalk, S., Lin, M. C., and Manocha, D. *OBBTree: a hierarchical structure for rapid interference detection.* SIGGRAPH 1996, 171–180.
- van den Bergen, G. *Efficient collision detection of complex deformable models using AABB trees.* J. Graphics Tools 2(4), 1–13, 1997.
- Brinkhoff, T., Kriegel, H.-P., and Seeger, B. *Efficient processing of spatial joins using R-trees.* SIGMOD 1993, 237–246.

## Dual-tree methods in machine learning

- Deng, K. and Moore, A. W. *Multiresolution instance-based learning.* IJCAI 1995, 1233–1239. (mrkd-tree)
- Gray, A. G. and Moore, A. W. *'N-body' problems in statistical learning.* NIPS 13, 2000.
- Ram, P., Lee, D., March, W. B., and Gray, A. G. *Linear-time algorithms for pairwise statistical problems.* NIPS 22, 2009.
- Curtin, R. R., March, W. B., Ram, P., Anderson, D. V., Gray, A. G., and Isbell, C. L. *Tree-independent dual-tree algorithms.* ICML 2013, PMLR 28(3), 1435–1443.
- Curtin, R. R., Lee, D., March, W. B., and Ram, P. *Plug-and-play dual-tree algorithm runtime analysis.* J. Mach. Learn. Res. 16(101), 3269–3297, 2015.
